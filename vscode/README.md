# DeskBuddy for VS Code

A friendly animated character lives in the **DeskBuddy** view of the Explorer sidebar and reminds you to drink water, take breaks, stretch and rest your eyes.

- Three free characters: Mika, Arjun and Zoe
- Water, break, stretch, eye rest and one custom reminder, each with its own interval
- Hanging charms (evil eye, lemon and chilli, drishti mask, hamsa hand, Webby the spider) that swing from the top of the view
- Timed reminders for a set time ("Meeting at 11:00"), with an optional warning 5 to 15 minutes before
- Snooze, or skip a reminder for the rest of the day
- Focus timer that tells you when to take a break and when to get back to work
- Quiet hours and an optional daily summary
- Water drop, rocket, balloon and confetti animations in the view
- Desk pet mode: the buddy strolls around its view
- If the view is hidden, reminders arrive as normal VS Code notifications

The DeskBuddy view has buttons in its header (hover over the view title): choose character, choose hanging charm, try a reminder, and settings.

Commands (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+P): `DeskBuddy: Try a Reminder Now`, `DeskBuddy: Choose Character`, `DeskBuddy: Choose Hanging Charm`, `DeskBuddy: Add Timed Reminder`, `DeskBuddy: Remove Timed Reminder`, `DeskBuddy: Start or Stop Focus Timer`, `DeskBuddy: Pause or Resume Reminders (1 hour)`, `DeskBuddy: Preview Pro (Coming Soon)`.

Settings are under **Settings > Extensions > DeskBuddy**.

Pro (your photo as a buddy, trending characters) is not available yet; the preview only records interest.
