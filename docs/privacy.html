DeskBuddy privacy policy

Applies to the DeskBuddy browser extensions (Edge, Chrome-based browsers, Opera, Firefox), the VS Code extension and the desktop app. Last updated 19 November 2026.

Short version

DeskBuddy has no account, no analytics and no tracking. The only thing it ever sends is your email address, and only if you choose to join the Pro waitlist.

What is stored, and where

  • Your settings (name, character, reminder times and so on) and your daily and weekly reminder counts are stored on your own device, in your browser's extension storage, VS Code's storage or the desktop app's data folder.
  • In browsers that sync extension settings (for example Firefox or Edge sync), your browser may sync the settings across your own devices. That is done by the browser, not by DeskBuddy.
  • If you join the "Pro" waitlist, the email address you type and the Pro features you tapped are sent once to the DeskBuddy team by email, through the Web3Forms form service, so we can tell you when Pro launches. We use it for nothing else and never sell or share it. In Firefox you are asked for permission first. To have it deleted, write to support@zerotodeploy.in.

Why the extension asks for permissions

  • Access to all sites: only to draw the character at the bottom of the page. DeskBuddy never reads, collects or changes what is on the page.
  • Storage: to save your settings.
  • Alarms: a one-minute timer that decides when a reminder is due.
  • Notifications: a fallback for pages where the character cannot be drawn.

Remote code and third parties

DeskBuddy loads no remote code. It contacts no servers while it runs, except to send a Pro waitlist sign-up when you press "Notify me" (to api.web3forms.com).

Contact

Questions: support@zerotodeploy.in. Source code: github.com/JAFFERSHA/deskbuddy. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here.
