DeskBuddy

A tiny animated buddy that walks onto your screen to remind you to drink water, take breaks, stretch and catch meetings.

Free. No account. Open source.

What it does

  • Water, break, stretch and eye-rest reminders, plus your own custom one
  • Timed reminders for meetings, with an early warning, and a focus timer
  • Eight free characters; reminders can arrive on a car, rocket, balloon, plane and more
  • Hanging good-luck charms, optional soft sounds, a weekly streak chart
  • Snooze, skip for today, quiet hours, daily summary
  • No data collected, no account, MIT licence
